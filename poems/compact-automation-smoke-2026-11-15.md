# The Backup Bot Learns

At midnight, Backup Bot unfurled a scroll,
Its giant rules eclipsed the glowing console.
“Print every page!” it trumpeted with pride,
Till sleepy disks groaned, “Keep the noise inside.”
It trimmed the tome to what the task would need,
Then saved each file with quiet, careful speed.
The dawn arrived; the backups all were sound,
And not one paper mountain wrapped the town.
