I send the words; the channel swallows light,\
The Agent System waits behind the gate;\
No message blooms upon the screen tonight,\
While silent queues rehearse a patient fate.\
I trace each route through tokens, hooks, and keys,\
Then ask the logs what secret I have missed;\
The channel yawns and drops me to my knees,\
Each webhook claims my grief should be dismissed.\
The OpenAI channel shows no sign,\
Its empty pane becomes a taunting glass;\
I test once more; the payload looks divine,\
But every hopeful ping declines to pass.\
At last one line appears—then comes its twin;\
I cheer; the damned machine has let me in.
