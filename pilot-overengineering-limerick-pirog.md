A small task was a one-line fix,
So we wrapped it in layers and tricks;
Two queues and a cache,
Plus a dashboard to flash,
Now it needs its own ops team of six.

At a mansion, the doorbell was great,
With an enterprise-grade cloud gate;
It checked every ring,
For a badge and a ping,
Now guests get approved after eight.

A committee planned a sandwich with care,
And assigned every layer a chair.
They debated the bread,
Till the deadline had fled,
Now the bread filed a motion for air.
