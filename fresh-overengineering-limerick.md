A small bug whispered from beneath the floor,
We raised twelve layers, polished, bright, and tall;
Three dashboards guarded every creaking door,
And charts assured us nothing now could fall.
Each cog received a cog to watch it spin;
The watchers filed a ticket on the gears.
A board convened to let the fix begin,
Then mapped a five-year roadmap through the years.
The bug returned, still grinning, by and by;
Beside the fix, a kingdom overgrown;
We patched the patch; new patches learned to fly,
Till every layer claimed the work its own.
Keep fixes small; let needless layers end,
Or soon the patches will need patches, friend.
